AI Agents for Parents

A practical guide to useful help, clear boundaries, and human judgment

An introductory three-week course for parents who already understand chatbots and LLMs. Learn how agents use tools, how workflows are shaped, and how to choose appropriate autonomy and oversight in family life.

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish a conversational response from a goal-directed workflow that can use tools and take actions.
  • Explain how instructions, current context, memory, tools, and orchestration shape an agent’s behavior.
  • Design a useful multi-step workflow with relevant information, narrow capabilities, checks, and stop conditions.
  • Match permissions and human review to an action’s impact, reversibility, uncertainty, privacy, and affected people.
  • Evaluate an agent’s result against evidence and the original goal, then choose to accept, revise, retry, or stop.
